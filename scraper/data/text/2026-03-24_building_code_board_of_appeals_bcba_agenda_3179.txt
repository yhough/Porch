23.Pq a report I received directly from Fire Marshal Shepherd based on a March 10,2026,

24.1n a high-rise building, broken windows allow wind to enter the building freely and can
siguificantly accelerate fire development and spread to uncontrolled conditions. This
condition is particularly dangerous in taller buildings because wind pressure increases
with height and can drive fire and superheated smoke through hallways, stairwells, and
living spaces very rapidly.
25. When a structure with compromised windows experiences a fire, wind canpush flame
and heat through the interior, overcoming the capability of the fire suppression system,
and creating a situation where all combustible materials ignite simultaneously. This
phenomenon is known within fire science as wind-driven fire behavior, and it can cause
conditions that deteriorate quickly, often trapping occupants in upper floors or exit
pathways before they are able to escape or be rescued.
26. A fire suppression system may be understood by a lalperson as a qprinkler system- A
sprinkler system is designed to keep a fire from spreading until fire personnel can come
and fully extinguish it. A fire suppression system cannot prevent fire spread forwind-
driven fire behavior, as such fire behavior quickly becomes uncontrolled, blocking both
safe exit by building occupants and blocking first responders from getting into the space
where a fire is.
