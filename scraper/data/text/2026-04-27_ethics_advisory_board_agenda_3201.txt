Ethics Advisory Board
MEETING AGENDA
Monday, April 27, 2026 · 3:30 PM
Common Council Chambers, City Hall
108 E. Green St.
Ithaca, NY
1. Introductions
As this is the inaugural meeting of the Ethics Advisory Board, time will be devoted to orienting all
participants:
– Overview of the City's Ethics Advisory Board and its official charge
– Brief introductions of each member of the Board
– Presentation of the roles of all involved — both volunteer members and City staff
2. Parameters, Authority & Governing Charge
Discussion of the Board's scope of authority, operating parameters, and the legal or administrative
mandate that convenes the group, including any provisions not addressed within the official charge.
3. Purpose of This Initial Meeting
Review of the goals and intended outcomes for this organizational meeting, and any expectations
for subsequent sessions.
4. Review of Disclosure Statements
Review and discussion of the required disclosure statements submitted by Council members,
including any questions regarding completion, content, or process.
5. Schedule for Subsequent Meetings

Establishment of a meeting schedule, to the extent not already specified in the Board's charge,
including frequency, preferred days/times, and location.
