Background
The Ithaca Urban Renewal Agency (IURA) is seeking professional consultant services to provide technical
assistance on an independent contractor basis for development, administration, and implementation of its
community economic development program, including loan underwriting.
The IURA anticipates contracting for services for approximately $8,000.00 in 2026. Contract extensions of up
to three years with the selected contractor(s) may be negotiated in the future based on this RFP, subject to
applicable procurement regulations and the needs of the IURA. The amount of contracted services for future
years may increase or decrease.
The IURA is a public benefit corporation established as an urban renewal agency by New York State that
administers the City of Ithaca’s U.S. Department of Housing & Urban Development (HUD) Entitlement grant
program, undertakes urban renewal activities, and carries out community development programs.
The Agency’s mission is:
… to secure and manage resources to improve the social, physical and economic characteristics
of the City of Ithaca by expanding access to quality affordable housing, strengthening
neighborhoods and the local economy, and supporting other community development activities.
The IURA is committed to carrying out its mission in an equitable and sustainable manner, and
to build and maintain an inclusive community with the input of the people and organizations we
serve.
The Agency’s economic development programs include direct loan assistance to small businesses and
sale/lease of surplus real property. Priority services sought are (1) structuring economic development and
housing projects for feasibility and (2) underwriting loan applications.
Scope of Services
The firm or individual will be expected to provide the following consultant services:
1. Community Development Lending: Underwriting and advice on management of loan portfolio, including
but not limited to:

of economic development activities
• Advice on loan security and collateral
• Advice on loan agreements and promissory notes
• Advice on project structuring, including identification of other loan products where appropriate
• Advice on private-public partnership structuring agreements
• Advice on collection in event of a loan default
• Advice on record-keeping and job reporting

2. CDBG: Advice on how to best utilize CDBG resources to achieve IURA community development
objectives, including but not limited to:
• Compliance with CDBG regulations and requirements
• Project structuring, including subsidy layering
• Management of Program Income
• Best practices
• Identification of match financing sources
• Funding agreements

3. HOME: Advice on how to best to utilize HOME resources to achieve IURA community development
objectives, including but not limited to:
• Compliance with HOME regulations and requirements
• Project structuring, including subsidy layering
• Management of Program Income
• Best practices
• Identification of match financing sources
• Funding agreements
4. General Community Development Technical Assistance: Advice on planning, administration, and
implementation of community development projects, including housing, economic development, community
facilities, and public services.
